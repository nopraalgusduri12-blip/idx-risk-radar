Words on this page

Data score (0–100)
Where the stock ranks today among all 48, according to the models. 100 is the top. It is a rank, not a chance of profit.
Risk grade (A–E)
How bumpy the stock has been compared with the others: size of daily swings, worst days, biggest fall, sensitivity to IHSG, and how easy it is to trade. A is calmest, E is wildest.
Stop
The price where you sell to cap the loss. Here it sits 1.5 average daily ranges below the last price, rounded to a valid IDX tick.
Target 1 / Target 2
Prices to take profit: 1.5× and 2.5× the distance to the stop.
BEP (break-even price)
The selling price where you neither win nor lose after paying the buy fee, the sell fee and the 0.1% sales tax.
Lot
IDX trades in lots of 100 shares. "Risk per lot" is what one lot loses if the stop is hit.
Trend
Up: price above its 20, 50 and 100-day averages. Down: below all three. Mixed: anything in between.
Target-before-stop rate
Over the past year, how often this exact stop/target rule hit Target 1 before the stop within 20 trading days.
Traded value
Average rupiah traded per day over 20 days. Below Rp10 bn, orders can move the price and exits get harder.
